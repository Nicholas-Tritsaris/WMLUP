# W10MLUP Platform Overview

## Research Focus
W10MLUP is dedicated to the research and preservation of update mechanisms for Windows 10 Mobile. The primary focus is the FE3 update delivery system.

## Status
- **FE3 System**: Research in progress.
- **Update Discovery**: Functional mock-up.
- **CAB Management**: Analyzing encryption.

## Known Issues
- Update servers are largely offline; research uses cached metadata.
- Some newer branches (e.g., RS3) have unique signature requirements.
